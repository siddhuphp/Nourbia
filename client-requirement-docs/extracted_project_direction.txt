Purpose. This document provides the initial strategic direction for the Nourbia Foods website, e-commerce platform, brand presentation, product architecture, and future scalability. The website should support Nourbia's PROVE → BUILD → SCALE strategy while keeping the initial launch focused and commercially practical.

2. Primary Website Goal
The website will serve all three functions, with D2C sales as the primary initial function:
D2C E-commerce: individual products, Nourbia Boxes, nationwide U.S. ordering, mobile-friendly checkout, and future local pickup/delivery capabilities.
Brand Awareness: communicate Nourbia's mission, story, food philosophy, culturally relevant positioning, and product innovation.
Retailer / B2B Acquisition: create pathways for grocery stores, supermarkets, pharmacies, food-service organizations, churches and organizations, schools and institutions, distributors, and other buyers.
3. Brand Story and Business Information
Yes. Nourbia will provide the core brand story, company information, mission, positioning, founder story, product information, and other business content.
Why Nourbia exists
The importance of nutrition and culturally relevant food
The founder's personal connection to food insecurity and the importance of access to food
Haitian food as the initial foundation and proof of concept
Future expansion into African, Latin American, Caribbean, and other culturally relevant foods
Food processing and freeze-drying capabilities
Farmer and producer partnerships
Food-rescue / surplus-food sourcing where appropriate
The PROVE → BUILD → SCALE strategy
Tone: People-centered, trustworthy, modern, culturally respectful, nutritious, accessible, and commercially credible. Nourbia should not look like a charity or nonprofit. It is a for-profit food processing and distribution company with a strong social purpose.

6. Initial Market
Primary launch market: United States, with the ability to serve customers across all 50 states.
Initial customer segments include:
Haitian diaspora
Caribbean communities
African diaspora
Latin American communities
Immigrant and multicultural households
Refugee communities
Families seeking culturally familiar foods
Consumers interested in nutritious shelf-stable foods
Retailers serving multicultural communities
Long-term market: Canada, the Caribbean, Latin America, Africa, and additional international markets. The initial website should be U.S.-optimized but international-ready.
7. Competitors and Reference Brands
Nourbia does not have one direct competitor because its model combines several categories. The following brands are reference points for specific aspects of the customer experience, sourcing model, food presentation, or category positioning:
Too Good To Go: Food-rescue / surplus-food concept and consumer engagement
Thrive Market: E-commerce and food-shopping experience
Misfits Market: Food accessibility and alternative sourcing
Patagonia Provisions: Purpose-driven food branding
Harvest Right: Freeze-drying education and consumer product positioning
Whole Foods Market: Retail presentation, product categorization, and food discovery
Instacart: Grocery purchasing, pickup, and delivery experience
Important: Nourbia should not visually or strategically copy these brands. They are reference points only.
8. Nourbia's Differentiation
Nourbia combines: culturally relevant food + nutrition + food processing + freeze-drying + food access + D2C sales + B2B + retail distribution.
Culturally Relevant Food • Nutrition • Access • Processing • Distribution
9. Recommended Website Architecture
The website should not be designed simply as a Haitian food store. Haitian food is the initial foundation and proof of concept, while Nourbia is being built as a broader culturally relevant food platform.

10. Strategic Direction for the Web Team
Build for today's launch, but architect for tomorrow's scale. The initial website should be simple enough to launch efficiently while providing a strong foundation for expanding products, categories, retailers, pickup locations, delivery partners, multilingual customer support, and international markets.
